# How SoniaGenix® Uses SI

## Our Position

At SoniaGenix®, SI supports human expertise. It does not replace it.

Human God Given Intelligence + Artificial Super Intelligence = Beauty Intelligence.

SkinGPT bai SoniaGenix® and SknGuru are built on more than 25 years of clinical and aesthetic experience. SI helps us share that knowledge with more people. A real person stays responsible for what it says.

## We Trust You

You know your skin. You make your own choices.
Our tools give you clear information so you can decide what is right for you.
We do not use SI to pressure you into a purchase.

## Education First

SkinGPT and SknGuru are built to teach.
They explain ingredients, routines, and how skin works.
They give education and cosmetic guidance.
They do not diagnose medical conditions.
They do not replace your doctor, dermatologist, or nurse.

## Safety Built In

Our platform has safety checks that are always on:

- A filter that blocks ingredients on our prohibited list.
- A filter that stops exaggerated cosmetic claims.
- A trigger that points you to a medical professional when a question needs medical care.
