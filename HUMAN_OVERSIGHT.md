# SkinGPT bai SoniaGenix® — Human Oversight

At SoniaGenix®, human judgment remains central to the design and use of AI.

SkinGPT bai SoniaGenix® and SknGuru are intended to support informed decision-making, education, personalization, and skin wellness guidance — not replace qualified professional judgment.

## Human-in-the-Loop Approach

Our approach emphasizes:

- Human review for higher-risk outputs
- Clear escalation when professional evaluation may be appropriate
- Defined roles and permissions for AI systems
- Ongoing validation of AI-generated guidance
- Responsible use of personalization
- Continuous monitoring for safety, accuracy, and unintended behavior

## Clinical Boundaries

AI-generated information should not be treated as a diagnosis, treatment plan, or substitute for appropriate medical care.

Where a concern may require clinical evaluation, users should be encouraged to seek qualified professional care.

## Human Accountability

SoniaGenix® believes accountability should remain with people, not autonomous systems.

AI should operate within defined boundaries, transparent permissions, and governance controls.

## Our Principle

**Technology should support human judgment — not replace it.**
