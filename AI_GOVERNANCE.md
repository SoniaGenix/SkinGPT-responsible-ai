# SkinGPT bai SoniaGenix® — AI Governance

SoniaGenix® is developing SkinGPT bai SoniaGenix® and SknGuru within a responsible AI governance framework designed to support safety, accountability, transparency, privacy, and human oversight.

## Governance Purpose

Our AI governance approach is intended to help ensure that AI systems operate within clearly defined roles, permissions, boundaries, and responsibilities.

## Core Governance Principles

### 1. Defined AI Roles
AI systems and agents should have a clearly documented purpose and scope.

### 2. Permission Governance
Systems should receive only the access required to perform their intended functions.

### 3. Least-Privilege Access
Access to data, tools, services, and external systems should be limited to what is necessary.

### 4. Human Oversight
Higher-risk outputs and decisions should remain subject to appropriate human review.

### 5. Safety Validation
AI-generated guidance should be evaluated for accuracy, relevance, safety, and unintended behavior.

### 6. Transparency
Users should understand when AI is involved and the intended role and limitations of the system.

### 7. Privacy and Data Protection
Personal information should be handled responsibly with appropriate safeguards and access controls.

### 8. Lifecycle Governance
AI systems, agents, permissions, and integrations should be reviewed throughout their lifecycle.

This includes appropriate processes for:

- creation
- authorization
- monitoring
- modification
- suspension
- decommissioning

### 9. Accountability
Responsibility for AI-enabled systems should remain clearly assigned to people and organizations.

### 10. Continuous Improvement
Governance practices should evolve as technology, regulations, security risks, and industry standards change.
