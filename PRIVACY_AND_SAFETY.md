# SkinGPT bai SoniaGenix® — Privacy and Safety

At SoniaGenix®, privacy and safety are core principles in the development of SkinGPT bai SoniaGenix® and SknGuru.

## Privacy by Design

Our approach emphasizes:

- Collecting only information needed for the intended experience
- Limiting access to authorized systems and roles
- Protecting sensitive information
- Using appropriate permissions and access controls
- Supporting transparency around how AI systems are used
- Avoiding unnecessary exposure of personal information

## Safety by Design

AI systems should operate within defined boundaries and undergo appropriate testing, validation, and monitoring.

Our safety approach includes:

- Human oversight for higher-risk situations
- Clear limits on AI-generated guidance
- Escalation when professional evaluation may be appropriate
- Ongoing review for inaccurate, unsafe, or unintended outputs
- Responsible use of AI tools, agents, and permissions
- Continuous improvement as technology and risks evolve

## Data and Access Governance

AI systems and agents should receive only the information and permissions necessary to perform their intended function.

This includes:

- Least-privilege access
- Defined roles and responsibilities
- Permission governance
- Lifecycle controls
- Monitoring for unintended access or behavior

## User Trust

Users should be able to understand when they are interacting with AI and the intended purpose and limitations of the technology.

SoniaGenix® believes responsible innovation requires transparency, accountability, privacy, safety, and respect for the individual.
